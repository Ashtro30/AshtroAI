# Meridian Labs Employee Handbook

Meridian Labs is a 120-person IoT and embedded-systems company headquartered in Bengaluru. This handbook summarises the policies that apply to all full-time employees.

## Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to **3 days per week**. The remaining days are spent in the office to support collaboration and hardware lab access.

Core collaboration hours are **10:00-15:00 IST**. During core hours employees must be reachable on Slack and available for meetings. Outside core hours, schedules are flexible.

Fully remote arrangements (more than 3 remote days per week) require written approval from a VP and are reviewed every six months.

## Leave Policy

- Annual leave: 24 days per calendar year.
- Sick leave: 12 days per calendar year; a medical certificate is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.
- Carry-over: a maximum of 8 unused annual leave days may roll into the next year.
- Parental leave: 26 weeks for the primary caregiver and 4 weeks for the secondary caregiver.

## Learning and Development

Every employee receives a learning budget of **INR 50,000 per calendar year** for courses, certifications, books and conference tickets. Unused budget does not roll over. Requests above INR 15,000 need manager approval before purchase.

Employees who complete an approved certification receive a one-time bonus of INR 5,000.

## Expenses and Equipment

- Meals while travelling for work: up to INR 1,500 per day.
- Domestic flights: economy class, booked at least 7 days in advance.
- Laptops are refreshed every 36 months.
- A home-office stipend of INR 10,000 is paid once, when an employee first adopts a hybrid schedule.

## On-Call Compensation

Engineers on the on-call rota receive a stipend of INR 2,000 per on-call week, plus compensatory time off for any SEV1 incident handled between 22:00 and 06:00 IST.
